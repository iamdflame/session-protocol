SESSION lets you place an order in a tokenized stock at any hour and have it fill at the NYSE open or close, at the price the bell printed. It is the same price for everyone in that bell.

NVIDIA's price is made six and a half hours a day, but its token trades on Solana all 168 hours a week, and most of that volume comes in the hours the price isn't being made. Those are the hours pools are thinnest. SESSION gathers orders into a cross that fills at the bell's own print. The print's signature is verified on-chain before a single digit is used.
- Buyers and sellers net against each other for free.
- Market makers fill any imbalance, at a fee a standing backstop caps.
- Every cross gets a receipt, with what a Jupiter swap would have given at the same moment beside it.

Everything in this video was recorded on devnet at the real NYSE open on 25 September 2026:
- NVDA's opening print, $225.82, was posted and verified on-chain.
- The cross priced, cleared and settled every order.
- Its receipt compares the fill with the Jupiter quote at the bell: sellers got 37.16 bp more than the swap, and buyers 33.99 bp less. The pool was cheaper than the stock that morning, and the receipt says so.
- Both issuer-power drills passed, a mint pause and a multiplier change, with every escrow at zero.
