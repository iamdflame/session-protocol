## If a judge pushes back

**"Isn't 100% purity circular — you defined the residual as what's left?"**
Yes, which is why the product isn't about driving it to 100%. You strip the
bands you didn't mean and *keep* the ones you did. The output that matters is
the book and the correlation, both of which are measured, not defined.

**"Six months of thin on-chain data is a weak risk model."**
Correct, and it's stated in the README. PC1 explains only 23% of variance,
well below a traditional equity model, because these tokens trade 24/7 against
underlyings that trade 6.5 hours a day — that gap is real idiosyncratic noise.
The model is honest about seeing only what it has seen.

**"Underweighting isn't shorting."**
Also correct. It's the deliberate trade: it keeps every leg a swap, so the
position is executable today with no lending venue. A view needing true short
exposure is expressed as far as a long-only book allows, and the residual says so.
